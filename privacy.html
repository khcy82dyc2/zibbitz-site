Privacy

What this site, its contact form and the game keep, why, for how long, and how to have it removed. It is written in plain words and was checked against what is actually built on 7 October 2026. Zibbitz isn't open to players yet, and this page will be read again, line by line, before it is.

Who looks after it

Zibbitz is made by one independent creator, with an AI (Claude) doing most of the building. Your Roblox account, and everything Roblox itself keeps about you, is Roblox's to look after under Roblox's own privacy policy. This page covers only what Zibbitz keeps.

Still to come before launch: who exactly is responsible for the data Zibbitz keeps, and the legal grounds for each use of it. Until that is written, we don't claim either.

This site

  • We run no analytics, set no cookies, run no script and collect nothing through these pages. See Cookies.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the internet address of everyone who visits a site it hosts, for security (GitHub's page about it, and its privacy statement). Those logs are GitHub's; we don't receive them.

The contact form

The form isn't open yet. When it is, it will be reached from the grown-ups, organisations and creators pages, for people aged 18 or over, and the same note will stand beside it:

  • What is kept: your email, your message, that you ticked the "18 or over" box, which page you came from and when.
  • Why: so a person can read your message and, if it needs one, answer it by hand. An AI (Claude) sorts messages for the creator and never replies. Nothing is sold or shared, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • How long: junk is deleted 7 days after it is sorted; everything else 90 days after it is read, unless a conversation with you is still going.
  • Deleting it sooner: write again from the same address and say so; everything from that address is deleted.
  • To stop floods of messages, a scrambled code made from your connection's address is kept for one day. The address itself is never kept.
  • A message that seems to come from a child is deleted without a reply. A message that suggests a child may be in danger is passed to the creator at once (see Safeguarding).
  • The form runs on Cloudflare, which carries each message to us; how Cloudflare handles the connection itself is in its privacy policy.

The game

  • It asks for nothing about who a player is: no real name, age, email, address, school, photo or voice. It does not read where in the real world a player is, not even their country.
  • It keeps what it needs to remember a character (their level, their things, their pets, what they built), saved under the player's Roblox account number, as every Roblox game's saves are.
  • Nothing is kept in secret. Every part of the game declares each thing it saves about a player, in a sentence of plain words, and the player can read it in the game, in the Diary in their Journal.
  • Nothing about one player leaves the game. What leaves are totals of many players together (for example, how smoothly the game runs on each kind of device), with no names or account numbers, so nobody can be picked out.
  • A broken-thing report from the ladybird button keeps where in the game it happened, the kind of device, and which errors the game saw, with no name or account number.
  • A save is kept so that a character is still there when its player comes back. It is removed when a deletion request reaches us (below).

Seeing it, and having it deleted

  • To see what the game keeps, open the Diary in the game's Journal.
  • To have it deleted, use Roblox's own data privacy request (from Roblox's Support page). Roblox passes these requests on to the games a player has played, and a tool built for them removes the player's save and their entries in records shared with others, such as lines they wrote in other players' guestbooks.
  • Two limits, plainly: another player's own save may still hold this player's account number where it records something the two did together, and Roblox keeps old versions of removed records for 30 days before they are gone.
  • Anything else: ask through the contact form once it is open.
