For creators

Zibbitz is meant to grow with ideas from the people who play it: a creature, a place, a small story, a whole world. This page says how that is planned to work. None of it is open yet. We describe it now so you can see the rules before there is anything to join.

From an idea to the world

  1. You share the idea inside the game. That will be the only way in: not this website, not email, not a message to anyone. What you write passes Roblox's own text filter, like every word players write in the game.
  2. A fair machine judge reads it. The judge sees the idea, not who sent it. It scores every idea against the same rules, which will be public, and it gives a reason you can read, whether the answer is yes or not yet. It also checks that the idea isn't copied from someone else's game, book or film.
  3. The creator of Zibbitz approves it. For now, a person approves everything that goes into the world. The plan is for the judge to decide later, with the creator keeping the right to say no, and every such no published with its reason.
  4. Your credit goes in with it. If your idea becomes part of the world, the world says it was yours, inside the game, where players meet it.

This isn't open yet. When it is, this page will say how to find it in the game.

What it will never ask of you

  • Your real name, your age, where you live, or a way to reach you outside the game.
  • Robux or money. Nothing you buy makes an idea more likely to pass or be read sooner.

What makes a good idea here

  • It is your own. Inspired by something you love is welcome; copied from it is not.
  • It fits everyone who plays, children included: no romance, nothing cruel, nothing that sells luck or growth.
  • It is something to do, not only something to look at. The world is built around what people can try in it.
  • Monsters in Zibbitz are stuck, not evil. A creature idea is best when it has a habit the world could help it out of.
