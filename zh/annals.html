It is Year 241 of the Clock. 1032 people live in 55 towns, villages and camps across 31 lands (1 found since the clock was lit). No custom has become binding yet. Every line below happened by itself.

钟前
Before the Clock

现实时间2026年10月4日至18日
4 to 18 October 2026, real time

  • 4 Oct: 旧世界“无骑士”被收作回忆，奇比兹在原地从零开始。
    The old world, Knightless, was set aside as a memory, and Zibbitz began from nothing in the same place.
  • 4–5 Oct: 炉边镇立了起来：一座围着大炉火的镇子，有井，有摊子，有最早的居民，还有田野里的小动物。
    Hearthward was raised: a town round a great hearth, its well, its stalls, its first people and the creatures of its fields.
  • 5–9 Oct: 世界有了自己的规矩：怪物是要安抚的循环，家会等主人回来，路是人走出来的，愿望种在井边。
    The world learned its own laws: monsters as loops to be calmed, homes that wait for their owners, paths worn by walking, wishes planted by wells.
  • 10–14 Oct: 炉边镇周围一口气长出了近三十片土地，每片都有自己的镇子、居民、动物、植物和地标，连成一个没有边的圆世界。
    The lands were raised round Hearthward, near thirty of them, each with its own town, people, creatures, plants and landmark, joined into one round world with no edges.
  • 17 Oct: 第一批访客来了，待了几分钟，发现没什么回应他们，就走了。世界又请四十八位想象中的访客排练了一遍，他们也都走了，原因一样。
    The first visitors came, stayed a few minutes, found nothing that answered them, and left. The world was rehearsed with forty-eight imagined visitors, who all left too, for the same reasons.
  • 17–18 Oct: 炉边镇学会了打招呼：每个广场都有毛茸茸的小家伙，镇上的人第一次开口问好，还有一颗会醒来变成伙伴的种子。
    Hearthward learned to greet: woolly creatures in every square, a townsperson's first hello, a seed that wakes into a companion.
  • 18 Oct, 22:32 UTC: 世界的钟点亮了。从这一刻起，不管有没有人看，世界都按自己的年头过日子：现实里一小时，世界里二十年。
    The clock was lit. From here on the world lives its own years, twenty for every real hour, whether anyone is watching or not.
