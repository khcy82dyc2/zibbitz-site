Terms

Short terms in plain English, for this site and, once it opens, the game. Fuller terms will be written before Zibbitz opens to players.

This site

  • It is a preview. Things here may change, and some may turn out to be wrong; we fix what we find.
  • The words on it are Zibbitz's own. The press kit on the organisations page is there to be quoted.
  • Links to other websites are there to help; those websites are their owners', not ours.

Playing Zibbitz

  • Zibbitz is a game on Roblox. Roblox's Terms of Use and its Community Standards apply to everyone who plays it.
  • Nothing in the game is sold for Robux today. If anything ever is, it will be for looks only, its price shown up front, never a random item, and never something that makes a character stronger.
  • Nothing in the game can be turned into real money, and we never pay real money out for anything in it.
  • The world will keep changing. We never make a change that takes away a player's progress or the things they have.
  • Ideas for the world can't be sent yet; the creators page says how that is planned.

The contact form

  • Once it opens, it is for people aged 18 or over. Children can ask a grown-up to write for them.
  • Please don't send anything abusive, and nothing that names or describes a child beyond what is needed to keep them safe.
  • Every message is read, but not every message gets an answer. Privacy says what is kept and for how long.

Who we are not

Zibbitz is not made or endorsed by Roblox or Anthropic.

When these terms change

Each change is dated in the dev log.
