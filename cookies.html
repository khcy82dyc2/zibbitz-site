Cookies

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Its pages run no script at all, so there is no cookie banner and nothing to accept or refuse.

  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels, and nothing loaded from another website, not even fonts.
  • The host, GitHub Pages, sent no cookie when we checked on 7 October 2026. It does log visitors' internet addresses for security; Privacy says more.
  • The contact form, once it opens, sets no cookie either. To stop floods of messages it keeps a scrambled code made from your connection's address for one day.
  • Zibbitz itself runs inside Roblox. Cookies on Roblox's website and app are Roblox's own, described in Roblox's policies. Zibbitz adds none.

If this ever changes, the change is dated in the dev log, and this page will say so before anything is set.
