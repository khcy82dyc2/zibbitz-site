Hello, Sparket!

In Zibbitz, a new player is called a Sparket. Here is what waits for you.

The world

You can try things on much of what you see. Climb a tree. Water the flowers. Plant seeds in the garden. Help mend an old bench, or put a stone on a little tower. Some of what you do stays there, for everyone who comes after you.

Never tell anyone your real name, your school or where you live. If someone makes you feel bad or unsafe, tell a grown-up you trust, and use Roblox's Report button.
