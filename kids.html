Hello, Sparket!

In Zibbitz, a new player is called a Sparket. Here is what waits for you.

The world

You can try things on much of what you see. Climb a tree. Water the flowers. Plant seeds in the garden. Help mend an old bench, or put a stone on a little tower. Some of what you do stays there, for everyone who comes after you.

Pets

At level 2, something wriggles in your pocket. It's your first pet, and it follows you everywhere.

A pet grows into whatever you do together. When it is ready to grow, you can pick another way for it to grow. It's your pet, so it's your choice.

Pets love food you make yourself, like a Berry Jam Sandwich or Mushroom Soup. Press the hammer button to make it.

Want more pets? The pet keeper in town has some for Gold you find in the game. Some pets hatch from an egg a big boss leaves behind, and some just turn up out in the world.

Ranks

Everyone starts as a Sparket. As you play, your rank grows, about every three levels, all the way to Worldwright at level 60. There are 21 ranks.

Each rank gives you a little move of your own, like skipping a stone or turning a glowing key in the air.

A rank shows how you have grown. It doesn't mean you are better than anyone else.

How to join

Zibbitz isn't open yet. When it opens, a grown-up can help you find it on Roblox.

Got an idea?

There isn't a way to send us your ideas from inside the game yet. We are working on one, and this page will say how when it's ready.

If something in the game looks broken, tap the ladybird button and pick a picture. You don't need to type anything.

Be safe, be kind

Never tell anyone your real name, your school or where you live. If someone makes you feel bad or unsafe, tell a grown-up you trust, and use Roblox's Report button.

Want to ask us something?

Ask a grown-up to use the grown-up page.
