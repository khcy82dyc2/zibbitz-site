Accessibility

We want this site and the game to work for as many people as possible. We aim for WCAG 2.2 at level AA. We have not checked against it in full yet, so we don't claim to meet it. Here is what holds today and what we know is not done.

This site

What holds:

  • Plain pages with headings in order and no script, so nothing moves, flashes or times out.
  • Each page is marked with its language, so a screen reader can choose the right voice.
  • Text sizes follow your browser's own setting, and pages reflow on narrow screens.
  • Every link is written in words.
  • Every page has an English and a Simplified Chinese version, each written in its own language rather than translated word for word, and a link at the top goes between them.
  • The text colours were measured against the background: the main text is about 14 to 1, the small grey text at least 4.6 to 1, and the white banner text 4.95 to 1 (AA asks for 4.5 to 1).

Not done yet (on our list before launch):

  • Trying the site with screen readers, with a keyboard alone, and on old phones and tablets.
  • A full check against every WCAG 2.2 AA rule.

The game

WCAG is written for the web, and we use it as a guide for the game. Many of the game's buttons show a picture beside the word. A child can tell us something looks broken with the ladybird button by tapping pictures, with no typing. The game is written in English and Simplified Chinese, and it is being built for phones, tablets, computers and consoles. It has not yet been tried by players who use assistive technology, and we don't claim more than that.

Tell us

If something here or in the game is hard to use, a grown-up can tell us through the contact form on the grown-ups page once it is open.
