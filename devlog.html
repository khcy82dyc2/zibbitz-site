Dev log

What changed in Zibbitz and on this site, newest first, in plain words.

7 October 2026
Five short pages, linked at the foot of every page: privacy, cookies, accessibility, safeguarding (including what to do if a child is in danger), and terms. Each says only what holds today and names what is still to come. The grown-ups' page now also says that this site's host, GitHub, logs visitors' internet addresses for security, and the orange strip at the top is a little darker so its white words are easier to read.
The creators' page now says how sharing an idea is planned to work (from inside the game, read by a fair machine judge that sees the idea and not the person, approved by Zibbitz's creator, credited in the world), what it will never ask of you, and what makes a good idea here. None of it is open yet, and the page says so.
The page for organisations is now whole apart from the contact form: the principles Zibbitz is built by and the lines it never crosses, how it is made (what the AI decides, and what only its creator can approve), and a short press kit in words.
The page for organisations now says how Zibbitz's costs are covered, and maps what the game does to Roblox's rules, UK and EU data protection law, the ICO Children's Code, COPPA, the UK Online Safety Act and WCAG 2.2, each with what we checked and what we have not.
The grown-ups' page now says why Zibbitz exists, how it is made for playing together, which tricks it doesn't play on players (we checked each one against the game first), how to use Roblox's parental controls, and what the game keeps, in plain words.
The kids' page now says how pets come and grow, what ranks are, and that there is no way yet to send ideas from inside the game.
This site went up as a preview: a first page that asks who you are, and a path for kids, grown-ups, organisations and creators, in English and Simplified Chinese. Most sections are still to be written.
In the game (still on its test copy, not open to players): a ladybird button now lets players tell us something is wrong by tapping a picture, with no typing.
Also in the game: a change can now go to a few servers first. If those servers start running slower or hitting more errors than the rest, the change is taken back by itself.
